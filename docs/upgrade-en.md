# CoinsPay for LiteCart: upgrade

1. Copy the contents of the `public_html` folder of the new archive into the root folder of your
   store, as on installation, and let it replace the files of the module.
2. Open Modules → Payment → CoinsPay in the admin: the line under the panel shows the new version.

The settings, the order statuses, the job and the invoices stay as they are. Do not uninstall the
module before the upgrade: uninstalling removes its job and its link secret.

What changed in each version is in the changelog: https://github.com/coinspayapp/coinspay-litecart#changelog
